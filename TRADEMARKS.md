## What you may do without asking

- Share the official builds, unchanged, under their name: the installer from
  this repository's releases, the Microsoft Store copy, the itch.io page.
- Say truthfully that your work is based on, forked from or compatible with
  Gulf of Trump, in plain text, as long as nobody could take your work for the
  original or think the author endorses it.
- Use the name and the logo to write or talk about the game: reviews, videos,
  articles, wikis, streams.

## What needs another name

A fork or any changed version that you distribute or run for others must:

- use a name and a logo of its own that cannot be confused with Gulf of Trump;
- not be presented as the original, an official version or a successor;
- keep the copyright notices and the licence, as the AGPL requires.

For anything else, such as merchandise or using the name in a product of your
own, write to the author first: https://github.com/Darhaal.
